Final takeaway

C = Clarify → S = Structure → R = Recommend

1. Before I Start:
C = Clarify the context

Before asking “what data do they need?”, ask “what decision is this supporting?”

  • What is the objective?
  • Who is making the decision?
  • What risk are being evaluated?
  • What recommendation would help?

2. Before I Solve:
S = Structure the problem

Messy problems become manageable when structured.

Break it into smaller pieces — then prioritize.

3. Before I Report:
R = Recommend workable solutions with Next Steps

Make the solution executable, not just good on paper.

Every recommendation needs:

  1. Action someone can execute
  2. Owner and timing
  3. Risk and next check

The real question is not just “What is the problem?”

It is: What decision needs to be made, and how can we move it forward?
